Using The Fruit Tree Map - A Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1 - Navigating around the Map

To Navigate around the map simply click and drag you mouse to move around. Zooming in also functions with the mouses scroll wheel.

Step 2 - Selecting a Fruit Tree

When you are wanting to select a certain fruit tree. Simply zoom in untill a coloured icon appears in your desired location and click it. A pop up should then appear with Tree information and a Direction option

Step 3 - Getting Directions to a Fruit Tree

To get Directions to a certain Fruit Tree, simply select the tree and hit the "Get Directions" button. A pop-up will then appear asking for loaction permissions. Once allow is then pressed, the best posible route will then be shown on the map with instructions appearing in the top right. As mentioned in the about page, Your location data is not stored passed your interaction.

Step 4 - Applying a Fruit Tree Filter

To apply a filter simply click the drop down menu in the bottom left corner of the map and select a Fruit Tree type. The map will then filter results to your selection.
