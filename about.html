About The Christchurch Fruit Trail Project

The goal of this project is to develop a responsive map enabled web application that provides the users with data of all the publicly accessible fruit trees in the Christchurch area. There is also an aim to educate all ages about the types of trees grown around our and when they are in season. This application should give users easy access to a free regenerative food source which will be beneficial to the community and all who chose to use it.

Things to Remember

Only fruit trees that are publicly accessible are included on the map. All of the tree's showing have been gathered from Christchurch City Government Data. Users must respect private land at all times. Please do not pick fruit from trees that are not publicly accessible or available. Also please collect the fruit in a safe way that protects and does not damage the tree in anyway. Please also only take what you need, Do not take an excessive amount of fruit. This Website does not store any personal infomation past the point of its use. Your privacy is fully respected.
